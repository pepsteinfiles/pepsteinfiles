Can Man City be stripped of their titles?

Three of City's Premier League titles were won in seasons where the Commission found breaches. Whether they can be taken away is the question most fans are asking. Here is what the official documents do and don't say.

Updated 27 October 2026. Official sources only.

Which titles are in question?

The Commission's findings cover the seasons 2009/10 to 2017/18. City won the Premier League in three of those seasons: 2011–12, 2013–14 and 2017–18.

Source: Premier League statement on the decision (Premier League · 29 Sep 2026) · Original referral to the Commission (Premier League · 6 Feb 2023)

Can the Premier League strip the titles?

The official statements don't say. The Premier League's statement says the Commission has broad discretion over sanctions, including fines and points deductions, and doesn't mention titles either way. So for now there is no official answer to whether titles can or will be removed.

Source: Premier League statement on the decision (Premier League · 29 Sep 2026)

What does The 115 Petition ask for?

The 115 Petition asks the Board of the Premier League to remove the titles Manchester City won in the seasons where the Independent Commission found breaches of the rules. The counter-petition says the titles were won on the pitch and should stand. Both totals are sent to the league together, as counts, never names.
