Sanctions, appeal, timeline

What happens next for Man City?

The verdict is in. The punishment isn't. Here is how the rest of the process works, according to the Premier League and the club, and what nobody has confirmed yet.

Updated 27 October 2026. Official sources only.

How will the sanctions be decided?

The Premier League says sanctions will be decided at a separate, private hearing. It says the Commission has broad discretion over the punishment, including fines and points deductions.

Source: Premier League statement on the decision (Premier League · 29 Sep 2026)

When will Man City's sanctions be announced?

No date has been announced in the official statements (as of 27 October 2026). We'll update this page when the Premier League confirms one. Forecast it yourself on the board: Will sanctions be announced before 1 January 2027?

Source: Premier League statement on the decision (Premier League · 29 Sep 2026)

Can Man City appeal?

Yes. The Premier League's statement says the club could appeal the findings until Friday 2 October 2026, and that appeals go to an Appeal Board. City said it would pursue the appeal routes open to it.

Forecast the outcome: Will an appeal change the outcome?

Source: Premier League statement on the decision (Premier League · 29 Sep 2026) · Manchester City club statement (Man City · 29 Sep 2026)

Will the full decision be published?

A redacted Core Decision has been published. The Premier League says the full award, including appendices, will be published when it is able to do so.

Source: Premier League statement on the decision (Premier League · 29 Sep 2026) · Independent Commission: Redacted Core Decision (PDF · 29 Sep 2026)

What could the punishment be?

The official statements name fines and points deductions as options, and leave the rest to the Commission's discretion. They don't say whether titles could be removed or whether relegation or expulsion is possible. Fans are voting on all of these: points deduction, 30+ points, transfer ban, losing a title.

Source: Premier League statement on the decision (Premier League · 29 Sep 2026)
